HOME MAINTENANCE APP

A home maintenance reminder app that remembers for you.

HomeKeep is an iPhone, iPad and Mac app that keeps your home maintenance, reminders and appliance records in one place. I made it because the usual options, a notes app, a spreadsheet or a memory, do not nudge you when something is due.

What it does

  • Repeating tasks and reminders. Finish a task and it comes back on its schedule.
  • Saved checklist progress. Tick off individual steps and pick up where you left off.
  • Appliance records. Model and serial numbers, manual links, purchase dates and warranty reminders, kept together.
  • Completion history and notes. A record of what was done and when.
  • Custom tasks and multiple properties.
  • Home screen widgets and backup export.

What it does not do

HomeKeep does not currently include iCloud sync or shared household task assignments. There is no account to create, and Pro is available as a single payment if you do not want a subscription. Everyday planning works offline.

Keeping appliance and warranty details handy

When something breaks, you usually need the model number, the serial number and the warranty date at the same moment. Saving them when you set the appliance up means they are one tap away later. Warranty reminders are optional; always check the actual terms in your warranty documents.

How to do this with HomeKeep

  1. Download HomeKeep from the App Store and open it.
  2. Add your home and choose the systems it has.
  3. Pick your starter tasks and allow notifications.
  4. Complete tasks as they come due. HomeKeep logs the date and schedules the next one.
  5. Add your appliances with model, serial, purchase and warranty details.
  6. Export a backup now and then to keep a separate copy of your records.

Frequently asked questions

How much does HomeKeep cost?

Free to start with one home, 15 active tasks and 3 appliances. HomeKeep Pro unlocks unlimited tasks and appliances and up to three homes: $4.99 once, or a 7-day free trial then $2.99 per year. No subscription is required, since Pro is also available as a single payment.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everyday planning and saved records work offline. Opening online manuals needs a connection.

Can I share tasks with my partner?

Not currently. HomeKeep does not include iCloud sync or shared assignments, so consider that before you start.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac (macOS 14 or later), on the same App Store listing.
