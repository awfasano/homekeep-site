SEASONAL HOME MAINTENANCE

A seasonal home maintenance schedule, spring through winter.

Grouping home upkeep by season keeps the list short at any one time. You do not have to do everything at once. You just need to know what belongs to this season.

This is a general schedule. Climate matters a lot, so adjust it for where you live and follow your equipment instructions.

Spring

  • Book your AC tune-up before the first hot spell.
  • Look over the exterior from the ground for winter damage.
  • Check gutters and downspouts and arrange cleaning if needed.
  • Test smoke and CO alarms.

Summer

  • Keep the outdoor AC unit clear of leaves and debris, following the manufacturer's guidance.
  • Check under sinks and around appliances for slow leaks.
  • Replace the HVAC filter on schedule.

Fall

  • Book your heating-system tune-up before you rely on it daily.
  • Schedule a chimney inspection if you use a fireplace or wood stove.
  • Review the thermostat settings for colder weather.
  • Look for drafts around doors and windows.
  • Clear gutters of leaves, using safe methods or a professional for anything at height.

Winter

  • Watch for ice dams, drips and damp spots after cold snaps.
  • Know where your main water shut-off is.
  • Check alarm batteries and replacement dates.
Work on roofs, chimneys, gas appliances and wiring belongs to qualified professionals.

Frequently asked questions

What home maintenance should be done each season?

Spring and summer centre on cooling, exterior and drainage. Fall centres on heating, drafts and gutters. Winter centres on watching for leaks and cold-weather issues. Year-round items like alarms and filters repeat all year.

When should I get my furnace serviced?

Many people book it in fall before heating season. Ask a qualified HVAC contractor what they recommend for your equipment.

Does HomeKeep know my climate?

HomeKeep groups its starter tasks by season. Adjust due dates and add custom tasks to fit where you live.

Do I have to do every task?

No. You can hide tasks that do not apply to your home.
