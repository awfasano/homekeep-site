A note you can include

Here is a free maintenance checklist to help you settle into your home. Adapt it to your inspection findings, equipment manuals, and local conditions. You can print it or check items off online. HomeKeep is an optional paid iPhone and iPad app if you would also like recurring reminders.

Sharing permission

You may print, email, or link to these free HomeKeep worksheets for personal use and client welcome materials, including commercial real estate and inspection work. Keep the HomeKeep credit and source notes. You may add your contact details in the space provided on the welcome sheet. Do not resell the worksheets or imply that HomeKeep endorses your business.

The checklist complements your existing materials; it does not replace an inspection report, equipment instructions, or professional advice.
