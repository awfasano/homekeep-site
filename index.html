Who HomeKeep is for

Consider HomeKeep if you use an iPhone or iPad and want to keep recurring home maintenance, reminders, and appliance records in one place without a subscription. It is a good fit for new homeowners building their first maintenance routine and existing homeowners who want a record of what was done and when.

Use it to remember HVAC filter checks, return to a partly finished checklist, or find an appliance's model number and warranty date. Everyday planning works offline, and your home records are stored on your device.

HomeKeep currently does not include iCloud sync or shared household task assignments. If those are essential to your household, take that into account before buying.

Questions about HomeKeep

Is HomeKeep a subscription?

No. HomeKeep is a paid app with a one-time purchase and no subscription. See the App Store for pricing in your region.

Can I track appliance warranties and maintenance?

Yes. Appliance records keep model and serial numbers, purchase dates, manual links, warranty dates and reminders, and related maintenance together. You can also start with our free appliance record worksheet.

Can I save a checklist and finish it later?

Yes. Check off individual steps, leave a maintenance task partly finished, and return to your saved progress. Steps reset for the next maintenance cycle when you complete the task.

Does HomeKeep need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Everyday planning and your saved home records work offline. Opening external websites, such as online manuals, requires an internet connection. Export a backup to keep a separate copy of your records.

Can I try the homeowner checklists without buying the app?

Yes. Our first-year homeowner checklist and fall maintenance checklist are free to use and print, with no signup or app purchase required.
