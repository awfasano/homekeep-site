IN A FEW WORDS

What HomeKeep does.

Short description

HomeKeep helps homeowners organize recurring maintenance, save checklist progress, and keep appliance details together on iPhone and iPad. One purchase, no subscription or account required.

Long description

HomeKeep brings recurring home maintenance, reminders, and appliance records into one place on iPhone and iPad. Homeowners can see what needs attention, check off individual steps, pause a job, and return to their saved progress. Records keep model and serial numbers, manual links, purchase dates, warranty reminders, and related maintenance together. Completion history and notes help answer “When did I last do that?” Users can undo mistakes, choose their reminder time, and postpone a reminder without changing the task’s due date. HomeKeep is a one-time purchase with no subscription or account. Everyday planning works offline, and users can export backups of their records.

HomeKeep currently has no iCloud sync or shared household task assignments. Online manuals and other external links need an internet connection. Users choose where to save or share exports; Apple device backups may also include app data. Read the privacy details.
