APP

Account: No account required.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
HomeKeep helps homeowners organize recurring maintenance, save checklist progress,
and keep appliance details together on iPhone and iPad. One purchase, no
subscription or account required.

LONG DESCRIPTION
HomeKeep brings recurring home maintenance, reminders, and appliance records into
one place on iPhone and iPad. Homeowners can see what needs attention, check off
individual steps, pause a job, and return to their saved progress. Records keep
model and serial numbers, manual links, purchase dates, warranty reminders, and
related maintenance together. Completion history and notes help answer "When did
I last do that?" Users can undo mistakes, choose their reminder time, and postpone
a reminder without changing the task's due date. HomeKeep is a one-time purchase
with no subscription or account. Everyday planning works offline, and users can
export backups of their records.

FEATURES
- A Home Plan for recurring home maintenance and reminders.
- Individual checklist steps, saved progress, and a fresh checklist next cycle.
- Appliance model and serial numbers, manual links, purchase and warranty dates.
- Warranty reminders and related maintenance tasks.
- Completion history, notes, corrections, and undo for mistakes.
- Preferred reminder time; snooze a reminder without changing the due date.
- Multiple properties, Home Screen widgets, and backup export.

DATA AND SCOPE
Home records are stored on the device. Everyday planning works offline.
No account, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics in the app.
HomeKeep currently has no iCloud sync or shared household task assignments.
Online manuals and other external links need an internet connection.
Users choose where to save or share exports. App data may also be included in
Apple device backups, depending on device settings.
